## Using it in the car

Use CarPlay and Android Auto only when it is safe and in line with traffic laws. Keep your attention on the road while driving.

## Your data

Your library, listening progress and bookmarks are stored on your device. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, if iCloud sync is on, this data syncs between your Apple devices through your own iCloud account. Apple decides when sync happens; changes may take a while to reach another device, and when two devices change the same thing at once, the latest change wins. There is no sync between Android and Apple devices.

Because there is no developer server, we are not able to recover data that is deleted or lost with a device.

What Pro includes and its price are shown in the app before purchase. Basic playback, downloads and offline listening are free.

- Pro works on devices signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account. Purchases do not transfer between the App Store and Google Play.
