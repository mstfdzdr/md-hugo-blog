**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

## 1. Scope and developer

This policy explains how the iOS (iPhone, iPad and Mac) and Android versions of the **Rewava** podcast player, developed by Mustafa Dizdar, handle data.

## 2. Our approach to privacy

You do not need to create an account or provide identity or contact details such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Your library, listening progress, bookmarks, and preferences are processed and stored on your device. The app does not send this information to a developer-operated server; there is no developer server behind the app. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, if iCloud is turned on, part of this information is stored in your own iCloud account so it can sync between your Apple devices (see section 5).

The app does not display advertisements or integrate services for advertising targeting, cross-app tracking, usage analytics, or automatic error-report transmission. We do not sell your app data or share it for advertising purposes.

## 3. Information processed on your device and its purposes

The app uses the following information only to provide the corresponding features:

- **Library:** The RSS addresses, titles, artwork addresses, and episode lists of the podcasts you add, along with your folders and sorting preferences, to display your podcasts and their episodes.
- **Listening progress:** Your position in episodes, completed episodes, the current episode, and your Up Next list, to resume where you left off and continue replay rounds.
- **Bookmarks:** The episode, the second, and an optional name you give, so you can return to a bookmark.
- **App preferences:** Choices such as accent color, random-episode settings, and download preferences, to retain them between sessions.
- **Downloaded files:** Audio files of the episodes you download and cached artwork images, for offline listening and faster display.
- **An OPML file you select:** The subscription addresses in the file are read during import, to move your podcasts from another app.
- **Rewava Pro status:** Whether Pro is active, the trial start date on Android, and the last known Pro status for offline launches, to unlock Pro features (see section 6).

The app does not access location, contacts, the camera, the microphone, the photo library, health records, or payment information for its features.

## 4. Services the app connects to

As a podcast player, the app connects to the internet to fetch content directly from its source. These connections do not pass through a developer-operated server.

- **Podcast search (Apple):** When you search the catalog, your search term and your device's region (country) code are sent to Apple's public iTunes Search API. When you paste an Apple Podcasts link, the podcast ID in the link is looked up through the same service. This also applies to the Android version. Apple processes these requests under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Podcast publishers:** RSS feeds, episode audio files, and artwork are downloaded from servers chosen by the person or organization that publishes the podcast. As with any internet connection, these servers can see your IP address and standard request information, which may include device and app details. Publishers and hosting services may process this information, for example for listening statistics, under their own policies.
- **Unencrypted connections:** Some older shows are only available over HTTP. The app tries a secure HTTPS connection first and falls back to the original HTTP address if that fails. HTTP connections are not encrypted.
- **iCloud (Apple devices only):** Apple's CloudKit service is used for sync (see section 5).
- **App Store and Google Play:** To check your Pro purchase and trial status (see section 6).

Podcast content belongs to its publishers; the app does not create, host, or moderate it.

## 5. iCloud sync (iPhone, iPad and Mac)

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device and allow Rewava to use iCloud, the app syncs the following between your iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account:

- Your podcasts, folders and season groupings
- Your position in episodes and completed episodes
- The current episode and your Up Next list
- Your bookmarks

This information is stored in a **private iCloud database** operated by Apple that only your Apple Account can access. The developer cannot access this database, see its contents, or delete it on your behalf. Apple processes this data under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and iCloud terms.

Downloaded audio files, download records and app preferences (such as playback speed, color and random-episode settings) are not synced; they stay separate on each device. The Android version does not use iCloud sync.

You can turn sync off for Rewava under **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**. With iCloud off, the app works with local data only.

## 6. Rewava Pro and purchases

Rewava Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; your card and payment details never reach the developer.

To find out whether Pro is active, the app reads your purchase and trial records directly from the App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play (Play Billing) on your device. No developer server or third-party purchase service is used to verify purchases.

- **7-day trial (iOS):** The trial is obtained as a free item on the App Store. Its start date is tied to your Apple Account and does not change if you reinstall the app.
- **7-day trial (Android):** The date you start the trial is stored only on your device.
- **Family Sharing (iOS):** Pro can be shared through Apple's Family Sharing, which Apple handles under its own rules.
- **Redeeming codes:** Offer and promo codes are redeemed in App Store or Google Play screens.

Apple and Google process purchase data under their own privacy policies. The developer can see the sales reports the stores provide to sellers; the stores determine what those reports contain.

## 7. Permissions and system features

- **Background playback and lock screen:** The title and artwork of the current episode are shown on the lock screen, in Control Center, or in the notification area.
- **CarPlay and Android Auto:** When connected, your library and the current episode are displayed on the car's screen. This information is shared between your device and the car; it is not sent to the developer.
- **Widgets:** Information about the current episode is passed to the home screen widget on your device.
- **Notifications (Android):** Notification permission is requested to show download progress and playback controls. If you decline, you can continue using the app.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Local records and preferences remain until you change or delete them, or until the app's local data is removed from your device. The developer cannot access these records on your device or in your iCloud account, or remotely delete them for you.

- **Podcasts, folders, and bookmarks:** You can delete them individually in the app. If iCloud sync is on, deletions also apply to your other Apple devices.
- **Downloaded episodes:** You can delete them individually or all at once on the **Downloads** screen. This only deletes the files on that device.
- **All local app data:** On Android, you can use the operating system's clear app data/storage function. On iOS, you can delete the app together with its local data. Merely offloading the app on iOS may retain its data.
- **Data in iCloud:** Deleting the app does not delete synced data in iCloud; your library comes back when you reinstall. You can delete this data in your device's iCloud storage settings (**Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage**).
- **System backups:** Depending on your device's backup settings, the operating system may back up or restore library and preference data. Downloaded audio files are excluded from backups. Those backups are not controlled by the developer.
- **Purchase records:** These are kept in your App Store or Google Play account; deleting the app does not delete them.

## 9. Security

Data is stored in the app's local storage and is subject to the device operating system's protections. Data synced to iCloud is protected by Apple's iCloud security measures. The app does not add password protection or app-level encryption to local records.

We recommend using a device lock and keeping your operating system up to date. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Support correspondence and other services

If you contact the developer by email, the address, message, and attachments you voluntarily provide are processed to respond to your support request. Support correspondence is separate from the app's local data handling.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to respond to and follow up on your request and, where applicable, to meet legal retention obligations. You can request deletion of this correspondence using the contact address below; information that must legally be retained may be excluded from deletion.

The App Store, Google Play, iCloud, Apple's search service, podcast publishers, and your email provider may process data for their own services under their own policies. This policy describes the app's data handling; it does not cover all processing performed by our website or those independent services.

## 11. Children's privacy

The app is a general podcast player and is not specifically designed for children. The app does not ask for age information, child accounts, or children's identity information. If you believe a support message has included a child's personal information, please contact us at the address below.
