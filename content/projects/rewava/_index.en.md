**Rewava** is a podcast player built less for discovering new shows and more for coming back to the ones you love. Shuffle a season and listen to it all over again, bookmark your favorite moment in an episode, and pick up right where you left off in the car.

## ✨ Features

### 🔁 Listening Again
- **Shuffle**: Play a season, a folder or a whole podcast in shuffled order; every episode plays once per round
- **Play All**: Listen to episodes back to back in release order
- **Random Episode**: Open a random episode from your library or a single podcast, optionally only from ones you haven't heard
- **Play with Earlier / Later Episodes**: Start from an episode and continue with the ones before or after it, staying within the season
- **Up Next**: Choose whether a new order replaces your current list or goes to the end, and clear the list with one tap

### 🔖 Bookmarks
- **One-Tap Bookmarks**: Mark the current second in the player and name it if you like
- **Bookmarks Screen**: See the bookmarks from your whole library in one list; tap one to play from that second

### 📚 Library
- **Adding Podcasts**: Search the catalog, or paste an RSS address or an Apple Podcasts link
- **Moving from Another App**: Bring your subscriptions over with an OPML file exported from Podcast Addict, Pocket Casts or Overcast
- **Season Folders**: Episodes are grouped by season automatically; create your own folders and move episodes between them
- **Sorting and Search**: Sort podcasts and episodes, and search episodes within a podcast

### ⬇️ Downloads and Offline Listening
- **Bulk Downloads**: Download a season or a whole podcast; the total size and free space are shown first
- **Wi-Fi Only**: Bulk downloads don't use mobile data by default
- **Downloads**: Manage downloaded episodes on one screen and delete them one by one or all at once

### ▶️ Player
- **Resume Where You Left Off**: The episode, position and queue are kept even after the app closes
- **Controls**: Skip back/forward 15 seconds, playback speed, lock screen and headphone controls
- **In the Car**: Browse your library and play or shuffle a season with CarPlay and Android Auto
- **Widgets**: Follow and control the current episode from your home screen

### 🎨 Look and Feel
- **Artwork-Forward Design**: Screens adapt to the colors of the podcast artwork
- **Accent Color**: Graphite, indigo, mint, coral, amber, or a color that follows the artwork
- **Light and Dark Theme**: Follows your system setting
- **iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android**: Dedicated layouts for large and foldable screens
- **Many Languages**: 46 languages on iOS, Turkish and English on Android

### 🔒 Privacy
- **No account**: Use it without signing up
- **No server**: Your library, listening history and bookmarks stay on your device
- **No ads or tracking**: No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services
