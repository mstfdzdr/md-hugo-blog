**PetTick** is a mobile app that helps cat and dog owners log their pets' daily care with as little effort as possible. Fed them, cleaned the litter box, went for a walk: one tap is enough. History, reminders, health records and statistics are all built from those logs.

## ✨ Features

### 🐾 Quick Care Logging
- **One-Tap Logging**: Food, water, litter, malt, walks, vitamins, grooming (coat/nails/teeth) and symptoms
- **Actions by Species**: Litter and malt for cats, walks for dogs
- **Detailed Logs**: Press and hold to add a past time, a subtype (dry/wet food, treats, scooping/full litter change…), an amount, a unit and a note
- **Undo**: Take back an accidental log right away
- **Personalization**: Hide and reorder the actions on the home screen for each pet

### ⏰ Reminders
- **Reminders Tied to Care**: Every day, on chosen weekdays, every few days or monthly
- **Several Times a Day**: Separate times for morning and evening meals, for example
- **Skip if Logged**: No notification if you've already logged that care today
- **"Done" from the Notification**: Log the care straight from the notification button

### 🩺 Health
- **Health History**: Vaccines and parasite care, vet visits, medications and lab results
- **Attachments**: Add images or PDFs to lab results
- **Next-Date Reminders**: Get reminded when the next vaccine or treatment is coming up
- **Health Info**: Microchip number, allergies and vet contact details
- **Symptom Log**: Vomiting, hairballs, diarrhea, loss of appetite, lethargy
- **Weight History**: Record measurements and see the trend on a chart

### 🛒 Food and Litter Stock
- **Purchase Log**: Record dry/wet food, treat and litter purchases with amount, price and store
- **Run-Out Estimate**: Estimates when your stock will run out from your purchase intervals and reminds you 3 days before

### 📊 History and Statistics
- **Activity History**: Every log by month and day; edit or delete
- **Statistics**: Feeding pattern over the last 30 days, litter cleaning over the last 7 days, and the change from the previous period

### 💾 Data Management
- **Stored on Device**: No account needed; your data stays on your device only
- **Export (Pro)**: Save all your data as a `.json` backup file
- **Restore**: Bring your data back from a backup file
- **Reset**: Delete all data with a two-step confirmation

### 🎨 User Experience
- **Light/Dark Theme**: Follows your system setting or can be chosen manually
- **Turkish and English**: System language or a manual choice

## ⭐ PetTick Pro

Basic logging is free. The free version includes 1 pet and 2 active reminders per pet (one time each). A one-time **PetTick Pro** purchase unlocks:

- Unlimited pets
- Unlimited active reminders and multiple times per reminder
- Data export

On devices signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account, you can reactivate Pro with **Settings → Restore purchases**.

**Where is my data stored?** Only on your device. PetTick has no account or cloud sync.

**How do I move my data to a new phone?** With PetTick Pro, create a backup file from **Settings → Export data**, then choose it with **Settings → Restore from backup** on the new device.

**I reinstalled the app and Pro is gone.** While signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account, tap **Settings → Restore purchases**.

**Reminders aren't arriving.** Check that PetTick notifications are allowed in your device settings and that Focus or battery saver modes aren't blocking them.

**How do I delete everything?** Use **Settings → Reset all data** or delete the app.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple ([reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)) or Google Play.
