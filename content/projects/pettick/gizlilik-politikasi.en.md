**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

PetTick ("the app") is provided by Mustafa Dizdar ("we"). This policy explains what information the iOS and Android versions of the app handle and why.

## Summary

- Your pet data stays on your device. We do not run servers that store it.
- The app has no account, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services.
- If you buy PetTick Pro, purchase information is processed by Apple or Google and by our purchase provider RevenueCat.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- **Pet profiles:** Name, species, breed, birth date, weight and an optional photo
- **Care logs:** Care type, time, and any subtype, amount, unit and notes you enter; symptom logs
- **Health records:** Vaccines and parasite care, vet visits, medications, lab results and the images or PDF files you attach to them; microchip number, allergies and vet contact details
- **Weight history:** Weight measurements and their dates
- **Food and litter purchases:** Category, product name, amount, price, store and purchase date
- **Reminders and preferences:** Reminder settings, quick action layout, language and appearance preferences

We cannot access this information. The app does not access location, contacts, the camera or the microphone.

## Photos and files

When you choose a pet photo or attach a file, the app asks the system for access and copies only the item you pick into its private storage.

## Notifications

Reminders, upcoming health treatment dates and stock run-out alerts are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No notification content is sent to us or to a push-notification service. Notification text may include your pet's name and may appear on your lock screen, depending on your device settings.

When you tap **Done** on a notification, the corresponding care log is created on your device.

## Data export and restore

PetTick Pro lets you export your data as a file. The export is created on your device and shared only where you choose through the system share sheet. The service you choose (for example iCloud Drive or Google Drive) stores the file under its own policy. The app does not encrypt backup files.

When you restore from a backup, the app reads only the file you pick in the file picker, and its contents replace the existing data on your device.

## Purchases

PetTick Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier, device and app information needed for the purchase, and your purchase history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it or the app is removed from your device.

- You can delete pets, care logs and health records individually in the app, and turn reminders off.
- **Settings → Reset all data** removes every pet, log, reminder and photo.
- Depending on your device's backup settings, the operating system may include app data in iCloud or Google backups and restore it to a new device. Those backups are not under our control.
- Backup files you have exported must be deleted separately wherever you saved them.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices

- Delete pet data inside the app or with **Reset all data**.
- Revoke photo or notification access in your device settings.
- Contact us for questions about purchase data processed by RevenueCat.

## Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data handling changes and revise the date above.
