**Last updated: January 18, 2024**

Your security matters to us. For this reason, the personal data you share with us is protected with care.

As the data controller of the mobile application you use, this privacy and personal data protection policy aims to inform you about which of your personal data will be processed and for what purpose, with whom and why the processed data may be shared, our data processing methods and legal grounds, and your rights regarding your processed data.

### Personal Data Collected, Method of Collection and Legal Basis
Your identity (such as name, surname, date of birth), contact (such as address, e-mail address, phone, IP, location), employment, social media and financial information, as well as visual and audio recordings, may be obtained by us through cookies and similar technologies, by automated or non-automated means, and sometimes from third parties such as analytics providers, advertising networks, search information providers and technology providers. This data is recorded, stored and updated, and is processed within the framework and for the duration of the service and contractual relationship between us, based on the condition of legitimate interest.

### Purpose of Processing Your Personal Data
The personal data you share with us is processed and updated, in a manner that is appropriate and proportionate to its purpose and for the duration of the contract and service, in order to: enter into contracts with you so that you can benefit from our services; fulfil the requirements of the services we offer in the best way and in accordance with the contracts between us; enable you to exercise the rights arising from these contracts; develop our products and services according to your needs and inform you about these developments; bring you together with broader service providers within legal frameworks; and fulfil obligations arising from the law (sharing personal data with judicial and administrative authorities upon request).

### To Whom and for What Purposes Collected Personal Data May Be Transferred
The personal data you share with us may be transferred to third parties, institutions and organisations in Turkey and abroad from which we receive and/or to which we provide services in order to carry out our activities, with which we have a contractual relationship or cooperate, and to judicial and administrative authorities upon request, provided that the necessary technical and administrative measures are taken.

### Your Rights as a Data Subject
Under Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), everyone has the right to apply to the data controller to:

- learn whether their personal data is processed,
- request information if their personal data has been processed,
- learn the purpose of processing their personal data and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose,
- know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom their personal data has been transferred,
- request the correction of their personal data if it has been processed incompletely or incorrectly,
- request the deletion or destruction of their personal data,
- request that the operations carried out under subparagraphs (e) and (f) be notified to third parties to whom the personal data has been transferred,
- object to a result against them arising from the analysis of the processed data exclusively through automated systems,
- request compensation for damages if they suffer damage due to unlawful processing of their personal data.

You can contact us at dizdardev[at]gmail.com to exercise the rights listed above.

### Contact
You are completely free to accept or not accept that only your necessary personal data is processed in accordance with this privacy and personal data processing policy, so that we can provide services in line with your requests. If you continue to use the application, we will assume that you have accepted it. If you do not accept, please remove the application from all your devices. For more information, please do not hesitate to contact us at dizdardev[at]gmail.com.
