### Summary
I'm Mustafa 🙂 I was born in Giresun in August 1993.
I was an average student, and although in my last year of middle school I had a chance to get into one of the
Anatolian high schools of the time, I chose Giresun 125th Year Anatolian Technical High School because of the projects they told us about.
There I specialised in Database Programming and worked with programming and database tools such as Visual Basic, C# and MSSQL. I also had the pride of
receiving an ***honourable mention*** at the Ministry of National Education's Project-Based Skills Competition with our project **Reymatik**, which was quite remarkable for its time. For details: [reymatik](/reymatik)

For my bachelor's degree I chose Karadeniz Technical University, where -since unfortunately I couldn't get into engineering- I studied Computer Education and Instructional Technology teaching.


I am currently doing a PhD in my field. For more details and work experience: [my LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mstfdzdr/)
